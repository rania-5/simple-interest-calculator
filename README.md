# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple web app that calculates simple interest from a principal amount, interest rate, and time period.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## How to use
1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate (%).
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Click **Compute Interest** to see the result.

## Example
Principal = 10000, Rate = 5%, Time = 2 years
Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 1000
